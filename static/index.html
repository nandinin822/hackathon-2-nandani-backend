Eldercare Safety Intelligence

Critical Eldercare High-Risk Scenarios

Falls in elderly care rarely happen without precursor events. AegisCare AI monitors key transitional hazard states—specifically unassisted bed-exits, sudden drops, and prolonged floor inactivity—providing preventive warnings before severe injury occurs.

Priority Hazard #1

Unassisted Bed-Exit & Chair Rise

Frail elderly patients frequently fall while attempting to stand from a bed or armchair without their walker or nursing assistance. AegisCare AI tracks the sudden vertical elevation of the trunk and hip displacement from resting coordinates.

DETECTION MECHANISM:

Trunk angle pitch > 65° + vertical hip velocity > 0.4 m/s while in designated bed boundary without walker landmark.

Critical Impact

Sudden Fall & Floor Impact

Rapid downward gravitational acceleration of the torso followed by complete floor stillness. Evaluated by our 2-checkpoint BiLSTM ensemble over 30-frame temporal sliding windows.

DETECTION MECHANISM:

BiLSTM Softmax probability > 0.65 across consecutive frames + horizontal torso bounding box aspect ratio > 1.4.

Distress Posture

Prolonged Floor Inactivity / Distress

Even if a fall occurs outside the camera's primary field of view, an elderly person remaining motionless on the floor for >45 seconds triggers an escalating distress code to nursing staff.

DETECTION MECHANISM:

Keypoint centroid elevation < 20% room baseline + motion variance < 0.02 across 45-second sliding accumulator.

Boundary Sentinel

Nighttime Roaming & Boundary Breach

Dementia and sleepwalking patients frequently wander toward exterior doors, unlit hallways, or stairwells. Virtual perimeter tripwires detect boundary breaches during curfew hours.

DETECTION MECHANISM:

Spatial intersection with virtual bounding zones during 10:00 PM – 06:00 AM curfew window.
